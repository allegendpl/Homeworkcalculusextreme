Copyright & removal requests.

We respect creators. Games and artwork must have explicit hosting or embedding permission. A permissive license is not a claim that we own someone else’s work.

How to request removal

If you own rights to material listed here, or are authorized to act for the owner, use the repository’s issue tracker with the title “Rights holder removal request.” A concise public issue can identify the material and ask the repository owner for a private contact channel before sharing personal information. The issue tracker is a contact route, not a designated DMCA agent.

  • Identify the copyrighted work and the rights holder.
  • Give the exact ExtremeCalculus game URL or artwork location you want reviewed.
  • Describe why hosting or embedding is unauthorized and provide supporting evidence or license details.
  • For a formal notice, include contact details, a good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized, a statement of accuracy and authority under penalty of perjury, and a physical or electronic signature.

Please do not post private addresses, signatures, or sensitive identity documents in a public issue. You may instead submit a formal notice directly to GitHub through GitHub’s DMCA procedure, which documents its private submission route and requirements.

What happens next

The repository owner should review the request, check the license or distribution permission, and promptly disable or remove disputed catalog entries while investigating where appropriate. If the claim concerns an external publisher embed, contact that publisher as well. We cannot promise a response time before an operator contact process is configured.

Operator responsibilities

Maintain a monitored contact channel, preserve permission records, and comply with applicable notice and counter-notice requirements. This page explains a removal route; it is not legal advice or a claim of statutory safe-harbor compliance.
