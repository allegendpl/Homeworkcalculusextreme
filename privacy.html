Privacy, without the fine-print maze.

Last updated: October 9, 2026. This policy describes the shipped static site. No analytics or ad network SDK is included by default.

What stays on your browser

We use localStorage for favorites (ec.favorites), play counts (ec.plays), your theme (ec.theme), and your advertising choice (ec.consent). These preferences are not sent by the portal to a server. If storage is blocked, settings work in memory for the current page visit but may not persist. Some games keep their own state when their sandbox or browser permits it.

Optional advertising

Portal ad scripts load only after you choose “Accept ads.” Declining leaves those scripts off and reserved slots empty. No advertising network is configured in this starter site. If the operator adds one, this page must be updated with the provider name, data collected, links to its policy, and legally required consent options. Some networks or regions require a certified consent-management platform; this simple banner alone is not a compliance guarantee.

Use the Privacy settings button in the footer to change your choice. Withdrawing consent reloads the page to stop active portal tags. This does not delete any cookies already set by third parties; clear them in your browser settings.

Game embeds and images

The five starter games and their artwork are served locally. Imported publisher games may load external scripts, ads, cookies, or other resources under that publisher’s privacy policy. External game iframes load only when you press Play and have accepted third-party advertising. Publisher thumbnails from a licensed feed load while browsing, sending your IP address and normal browser request data to their host; those hosts must also be documented when the feed is configured.

Hosting and offline support

GitHub Pages and any image or embed hosts can process IP addresses and request metadata for delivery and security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. The service worker caches the portal shell and recent catalog locally for offline browsing. It does not cache advertisements or external games.

Managing and removing your data

Reset play counts on Stats, remove favorites with the heart buttons, or clear this site’s storage and offline cache using your browser’s site-data controls. There is no server-side player profile to delete.

Questions

See Contact. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
