Small games, big possibilities.

ExtremeCalculus is an independent HTML5 games collection. No accounts, no downloads, and no portal backend. Pick a game, find your rhythm, and make it a favorite.

A collection with clear permissions

Our collection now includes 200+ games. The five locally hosted starter games can be distributed under the MIT license:

  • 2048 — created by Gabriele Cirulli (2014). Upstream source, MIT license, and pinned revision and modifications.
  • Neon Snake, Prism Breaker, Memory Mosaic, and Lights Out — original code and artwork by ExtremeCalculus contributors (2026), shared under the MIT license.
  • All card illustrations are original vector artwork of the actual game boards/mechanics; see the artwork license.

199 official publisher games

The expanded collection uses GameMonetize’s official JSON publisher feeds for QkyGames, Inlogic Software s.r.o., and Playtouch. GameMonetize’s FAQ explicitly permits embedding games on publisher websites, and its feed builder supplies game artwork, descriptions, controls, and official embed URLs.

These publisher games are copyrighted, not open-source. We use their authorized hosted embeds; we do not copy their game files or remove their branding, SDK, or advertisements. Titles and artwork were screened to exclude obvious unauthorized franchise lookalikes, logo games, and gambling titles. Source and permission information is shown on every player page. The reviewed selection is recorded in the feed allowlist and source notes.

External games load only after you press Play and accept third-party advertising. Availability, performance, publisher privacy policies, and network restrictions vary. Report a broken or questionable listing so it can be reviewed.

Built to grow, responsibly

The catalog supports thousands of entries, displayed in batches of 48. New games must come from an approved distribution program, a permissive open-source license, or written permission from the owner. Publisher games retain their official embeds and SDKs; the operator must verify rights and program terms before importing.

Respect your network

“Unblocked” describes lightweight browser games, not a promise of access on every network. This site has no proxy, bypass, disguised tabs, or filter-evasion tools. Follow your school, workplace, and network rules.

Your picks stay yours

Favorites, play counts, and theme preferences are stored only on your device. Read our privacy page for advertising and embedded-game details.
